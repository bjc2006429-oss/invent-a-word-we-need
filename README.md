## The word

**draftloop** (noun) — typing, deleting, and retyping a message until you give up and send 'ok' instead.

Example: "I draftlooped for ten minutes over a two-word reply, then just sent 'ok'."

Why it fills a gap: almost everyone has typed, deleted, and retyped the same message several times before giving up and sending something flat — and until now that loop had no name of its own.
